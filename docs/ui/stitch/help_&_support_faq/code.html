AI Stylist & Learning

How AI learns your style

expand_more

Rakufuku AI analyzes your uploaded wardrobe, previous outfit ratings, and current trends to create a personalized style DNA that evolves as you use the app.

Improving suggestion accuracy

expand_more

The more you rate your daily outfits, the better the AI understands your preferences. Try "hearting" outfits you love to prioritize similar looks.

Syncing & Events

Syncing with Google Calendar

expand_more

Go to Settings > Integrations and select Google Calendar. Once linked, Rakufuku automatically suggests outfits based on your event titles and locations.

Event-based outfit suggestions

expand_more

The AI checks the weather and dress code for your calendar events. For "Business Meeting" it selects professional wear, for "Gym" it selects activewear.

Account & Billing

Managing your Pro subscription

expand_more

Subscriptions can be managed through your App Store account settings. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
